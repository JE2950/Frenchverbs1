# La Cuisine de Chef Gaston

A Minecraft-style French verb escape game. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on GitHub Pages. There is nothing to install.

Food critic Madame Lagrange is coming to dinner, and Chef Gaston has locked every ingredient away. Each fridge, jar, sack and drawer opens only for a correct French verb.

- **Three kitchens:** l'entrée (salade niçoise), le plat principal (cassoulet de canard) and le dessert (éclairs au chocolat).
- **Ten ingredients per kitchen,** so 30 verb questions in all. With all ten, cook the dish on the middle counter and the next door opens.
- **Serve all three courses to Madame Lagrange.** Her star rating depends on how many verbs were right first time.

## Setup screen

- **Verbs:**
  - regular -er: donner, jouer, aimer, écouter, regarder, manger;
  - -ir: finir;
  - -re: vendre, perdre, entendre;
  - avoir, être, aller.
- **Tenses:** le présent, le passé composé (aller with être and agreement) and le futur proche.
- **Question types:** multiple choice, typed answers (with accent buttons), or both.

The verbs, endings and translations follow the French department's verb sheets.

Controls: WASD to walk, the mouse to look, E or a click to use, Esc to pause. On a tablet, use the on-screen stick and the USE button.

## Quick practice: Word Blaster

The **Quick practice** button on the title screen opens a typing game. Escaped snails float down carrying an English verb and its tense (*we finish · présent*). Type the French and press Enter to throw a baguette at them before they reach the counter. It uses the verbs and tenses ticked on the title screen, has accent buttons, and gives fewer points when only an accent is wrong. Three hearts, faster waves, a best score per computer, and a list of verbs to practise at the end.
