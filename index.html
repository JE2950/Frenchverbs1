Restaurant Le Gros Bouillon

PARIS · CE SOIR · 19H00

You are the new apprentice in the kitchen of Chef Gaston Grosbouillon. Tonight the most feared food critic in France, Madame Lagrange, is coming to dinner.

There is one small problem. Last week somebody ate all the butter, so Chef Gaston has locked every ingredient in his enchanted fridges, jars and cupboards. They open only for perfect French verbs. He says this is completely normal in France.

(It was Chef Gaston who ate the butter.)

HOW TO COOK DINNER

  1. There are three kitchens: l’entrée, le plat principal and le dessert. Each needs 10 ingredients.
  2. ✦ Golden sparkles mark a locked fridge, jar, sack or drawer. Walk up and press E (on a tablet, tap USE), then answer the verb question.
  3. For typed answers, use the accent buttons for é, è, à, ç and the rest.
  4. With all ten ingredients, cook the dish on the middle counter. The door to the next kitchen opens.
  5. Serve all three courses to Madame Lagrange in the dining room. Her stars depend on your verbs. And mind the snail.
